
# Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Fellowship Trends (2014–2026)

An interactive report visualizing historical trends in budget allocations, application volumes, funded proposals, and success rates for Marie Curie Fellowships (MSCA-IF and MSCA-PF/EF) from 2014 to 2026.

## Overview

This project analyzes the evolving competitiveness of the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships. It maps out three distinct phases and external shocks:
- **COVID-19 Disruption (2020–2021):** Shifting submission patterns and transitional fluctuations.
- **Framework & Umbrella Transition (2021):** The shift from Horizon 2020 (MSCA-IF) to Horizon Europe (MSCA-PF/EF).
- **UK Association & Generative AI Surge (2024–2026):** Re-entry of the UK into Horizon Europe in 2024 accompanied by an exponential increase in submitted proposals, driving success rates down to historic lows (~7.4% in 2026).

## Key Visualizations

1. **Budget Allocation Trends (M€):** Tracks the budget trajectory, including the dip in 2021–2022 and the substantial increase from 2024 onwards.
2. **Presented vs. Funded Proposals:** Highlights the divergence between stagnant funded slots (~1,150–1,700) and the post-2023 surge in applications (>21,000 in 2026).
3. **Chance of Funding (%):** Demonstrates the decline in success rates from ~14–18% historically to under 8%.
